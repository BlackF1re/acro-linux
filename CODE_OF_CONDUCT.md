# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in issues, reviews and project discussions should:

- Be respectful and welcoming; discuss code and evidence rather than people.
- Accept corrections, acknowledge uncertainty and credit other people's work.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory language, threats and personal attacks.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's identity or private data.
- Keep technical disagreements constructive and within the project's scope.

Maintainers may edit/remove abusive material or restrict participation. Actions
should be proportionate and explained where doing so does not expose private data.

Report concerns privately through a channel listed on the
[maintainer's profile](https://github.com/BlackF1re). If none is available, request
a private contact route without posting details about the people involved.
This policy applies to repository participation and project representation.
