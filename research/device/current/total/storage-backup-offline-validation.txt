Title: Offline validation of the private pre-experiment eMMC user-area backup
Date: 2026-09-01
Scope: host-side only; no phone block device was read for this validation.

Canonical private backup directory:
  /home/paul/xperia/backups/hikari/20260901T111716Z/hikari-golden-backup-20260901T190000Z

Acquisition provenance:
  The accepted backup was not made with scripts/backup-device.sh.  Session
  command provenance records one continuous, read-only phone-side stream:
    busybox dd if=/dev/block/mmcblk0 bs=4M 2>/dev/null |
      busybox split -b 1024m - mmcblk0.part-
  `split` consumed the ordered dd stream.  The resulting lexical sequence
  mmcblk0.part-aa through mmcblk0.part-ao is therefore byte-contiguous by
  construction; independent skip/count reads were not used.

Interpretation:
  The parts provide complete byte coverage of the accessible eMMC user area,
  including its MBR/extended partition map and p1--p15.  This was a live
  acquisition from mounted Android filesystems, so it is not asserted to be an
  atomic filesystem-consistent snapshot.  Sensitive partition bytes are in the
  private raw stream but were not separately inspected, parsed, interpreted,
  exposed, or committed to git.  Unexposed mmcblk0boot0, mmcblk0boot1 and RPMB
  areas are outside this coverage.
